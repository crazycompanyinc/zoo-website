API That Just Works

RESTful, predictable, versioned. Every endpoint returns JSON. Every error is actionable.

POST /v2/wallets

Create a new wallet for a user. Returns wallet ID, deposit addresses, and supported currencies.

GET /v2/wallets/:id/balance

Get real-time balance for a wallet. Includes pending transactions and available balance.

POST /v2/transfers

Transfer funds between wallets. Supports same-currency and cross-currency with auto-conversion.

POST /v2/deposits

Generate a deposit address or initiate a fiat bank transfer. Returns payment instructions.

POST /v2/withdrawals

Send funds to external bank accounts or blockchain addresses. Supports ACH, SEPA, wire, and crypto withdrawals.

GET /v2/transactions

List all transactions with filtering by wallet, currency, date range, and status. Paginated with cursor.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need blockchain knowledge to use this?

No. Wallet-as-a-Service abstracts all blockchain complexity. You interact with a simple REST API. We handle key management, gas fees, and chain-specific logic behind the scenes.

How does the Stripe integration work?

Connect your existing Stripe account via OAuth. We use Stripe for card processing and fiat payouts, while our ledger handles wallet balances and internal transfers. You get the best of both worlds.

Is this production-ready?

Yes. We're SOC 2 Type II certified, PCI DSS compliant, and process over $50M monthly. Our infrastructure runs on AWS with multi-region failover.

What happens if I exceed my API limit?

We'll notify you at 80% usage. If you exceed, we gracefully throttle (not hard-reject) and contact you about upgrading. Your service never goes down.

Can I white-label the wallet UI?

Yes. Our Growth and Enterprise plans include a customizable widget you can embed in your app. Full CSS control, your branding, your domain.

How do you handle security?

MPC (Multi-Party Computation) key management. No single point of failure. HSM-backed signing. All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3). Regular third-party audits.
